The Library holds a complete copy of Wikipedia — every article, fully searchable, stored
on the hub itself.

===

## Searching

Open **Library** from the front page and type into the search box. Results come from the
full text of the articles, not just their titles, so searching for *photosynthesis
chlorophyll* finds the right sections even when neither word is in a title.

## What's different from the real Wikipedia

* **No pictures.** The text-only copy is a fifth of the size, which is what makes it fit
  on the hub at all.
* **It's a snapshot.** The copy is from a particular month, so very recent events won't
  be in it.
* **No editing.** It's read-only.

## For homework

Articles can be read, copied and quoted the same as any other source. Cite it as
Wikipedia, with the date of the snapshot shown at the bottom of each article.
