The school now has its own offline hub. Everything on it works without an internet
connection — it runs on a small computer plugged into the school's own network.

===

## What's on it

* **Library** — the whole of Wikipedia, searchable, plus study material.
* **Ebooks** — classic literature, science and history, readable in the browser.
* **Games** — chess and checkers against a classmate, live.
* **News** — this page.

## How to get to it

On any phone, tablet or laptop connected to the school WiFi, open a browser and go to
**schoolhub.local**. There is nothing to install.
