Chess club now runs on the hub as well as in the library at lunchtime. Two students on the
school network can play each other live from any two devices.

===

## Starting a game

1. Open **Games** from the front page.
2. Choose chess or checkers, and press **Create room**.
3. You get a short code. Tell it to whoever you're playing.
4. They open Games, enter the code, and press **Join**.

Moves appear on both screens as they're made. If someone's WiFi drops out the game waits
for them for a minute or two rather than ending.
